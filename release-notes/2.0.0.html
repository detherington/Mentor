Mentor is now Pepper

Same app, new name. Your settings, permissions and recordings carry over; after this update Pepper renames itself in Applications and reopens once. Old .mentor recordings still open in the editor, and new ones are saved to Movies → Pepper.

  • Sign in to Orbis. Export to Orbis now uses Orbis sign-in in your browser, and personal access tokens no longer work. After updating, open Settings → Orbis and click Sign In once.
  • Everything lines up. Screen, webcam, voice, clicks and zooms share one timeline. Talking over a still final slide is no longer cut off, and stopping while paused no longer adds a frozen ending.
  • Edits stay with each recording. Trim, cuts, webcam layout and the recording's look are kept when you reopen it, and exports match the preview.
  • Your shortcuts. Set them in Settings → Shortcuts. Esc cancels the countdown. Soundboard hotkeys now need ⌘, ⌥ or ⌃, so rebind any Shift-only ones.
  • Safer recording. If capture stops or the disk fills up, Pepper saves what it recorded and tells you why. Quitting mid-recording offers Stop & Quit.
  • A faster editor. Smoother playback and dragging, and one ⌘Z undoes a whole drag. Edit Last Recording opens the newest recording, not the last one edited.
  • Double-clicking a recording opens it reliably, plus many smaller fixes.
