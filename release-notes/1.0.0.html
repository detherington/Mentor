Pepper 1.0

Record your screen, camera and voice, then polish the video in a simple editor and send it straight to Orbis.

  • Guided setup. A short walkthrough asks for each permission when it's needed and signs you in to Orbis. Run it again any time from Settings → Setup.
  • Quick polish. One click zooms into your clicks, writes captions and cuts long pauses. Every effect is a plain switch you can fine-tune.
  • Everything lines up. Screen, webcam, voice, clicks and zooms share one timeline, and your edits stay with each recording.
  • Send to Orbis. Sign in once, and finished videos go to your Orbis library with captions and a thumbnail.
  • Safer recording. If capture stops or the disk fills up, Pepper saves what it recorded and tells you why.
