Pepper 1.2.3

  • Share faster. When a video finishes uploading to Orbis, its link is copied for you, and there's a Copy Link button too.
  • Tidy up from the main window. Right-click a recent recording to open it, show it in Finder, or move it to the Trash.
  • Clearer errors. If an export or upload fails, Pepper says what went wrong and what to try, with Copy Details for the technical part.
  • Readable names. Editor windows show when a recording was made, like "Oct 2, 12:59 PM", and Orbis uploads get a matching title.
  • Menus and shortcuts. Check for Updates in the Pepper menu, and Export (⌘E) and Send to Orbis (⇧⌘E) in the File menu.
