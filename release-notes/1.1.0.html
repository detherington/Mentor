Pepper 1.1

Smoother from the moment you stop recording, and a Quick polish that never takes out the parts that matter.

  • Straight into the editor. Stopping a recording opens it in the editor. When the video file is ready, a small card offers Show in Finder.
  • Quick polish tells you what it did. It zooms into your clicks and writes captions, then shows what it changed. It no longer cuts pauses; use Cut long pauses in the Cuts row for that.
  • Smarter pause cutting. Cut long pauses leaves in quiet stretches where you're clicking or typing, so a silent demo stays in the video.
  • No surprise permission prompts. On macOS 26 and later, captions need no extra permission. On earlier versions, Pepper tells you before macOS asks.
  • Your displays by name. The source picker lists screens by name instead of "Display 2".
  • Familiar shortcuts. Settings (⌘,), Open Recording (⌘O), and Undo and Redo in the Edit menu.
  • Windows stay put. The editor, Settings and Soundboard reopen where you left them.
  • More private soundboard. Sound shortcuts only listen during a recording or while the Soundboard window is open.
