Pepper 1.2

Pepper now has a home: a main window and a place in the Dock, so it's always clear how to start.

  • A main window. Start a recording with one button, open any of your recent recordings, and check your camera, microphone and Orbis connection at a glance. It opens when Pepper starts and when you click the Dock icon, and steps aside while you record.
  • In the Dock. Pepper now shows in the Dock like any other app. Prefer it in the menu bar only? Turn on Hide Pepper from the Dock in Settings.
